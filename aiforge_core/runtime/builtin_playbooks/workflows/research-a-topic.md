name: research-a-topic
description: Procedure to research a question and produce a grounded, cited brief
triggers: [research, investigate, compare options, evaluate, spike, find out, brief]
source: builtin
---

1. **Sharpen the question**: what exactly do you need to decide/know? What would a good answer let you do?
2. **Gather from multiple sources**: docs, code, the web (web_search → fetch_url the best hits), prior decisions (memory_lookup). Don't rely on one source.
3. **Cross-check claims**: verify a key fact against a second source before trusting it; note version/date (things go stale).
4. **Synthesize, don't dump**: organize findings around the question; compare options on the criteria that matter (not a generic pro/con list).
5. **State a recommendation** with the reasoning and the trade-offs, plus what you're uncertain about.
6. **Cite** the sources so the reader can verify. For a durable choice, capture it as an ADR.
